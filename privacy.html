Privacy Policy

ViralCraft · Last updated: October 14, 2026

ViralCraft("the App") is a personal tool operated by Giudici Jacopo ("I", "me"). It turns videos owned by the operator into short vertical clips and publishes them, after manual review, to the operator's own social media accounts (TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube). This policy explains what data the App handles and how.

1. Who uses the App

The App is used only by its operator to manage the operator's own content and accounts. It is not offered as a public service and does not create accounts for third parties.

2. Data the App processes

  • Videos and transcripts: videos uploaded by the operator or downloaded from the operator's own YouTube channel, their audio transcripts and the clips generated from them.
  • Connected account data: when the operator connects a social account through the platform's official login (OAuth), the App receives an access token and basic account information (account ID, name or username, profile picture, and for Facebook the Pages managed and the linked Instagram account).
  • Publishing data: captions, hashtags, scheduled times, post IDs and publishing status returned by the platforms.

The App does not collect data about viewers or followers and does not use tracking cookies. A session cookie is used only to keep the operator logged in.

3. How the data is used

Data is used solely to generate clips, write post captions and upload or schedule posts to the operator's own accounts, as explicitly requested by the operator. Video transcripts are sent to Anthropic's Claude API to identify highlights and draft captions; Anthropic processes them under its own terms and privacy policy.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored on infrastructure controlled by the operator. Access tokens are stored encrypted. Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, except with the platforms themselves when publishing and with the API providers listed above.

5. Platform-specific information

  • TikTok: the App uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API only to upload videos to the operator's TikTok account.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram): the App uses Facebook Login and the Graph API only to publish Reels to the operator's Facebook Page and linked Instagram professional account.
  • YouTube / Google: the App uses YouTube API Services. By using these features you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

6. Retention and deletion

Videos, clips and publishing records are kept until the operator deletes them. Disconnecting an account in the App deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time from the settings of each platform. To request deletion of any data, write to the contact address below; requests are handled within 30 days.
